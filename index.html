Mirror — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Mirror is a personal, non-commercial smart-mirror application. It runs on one computer in its owner's home and is used only by its owner. It displays pictures from the owner's own Pinterest boards on a mirror screen.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Pinterest's own sign-in screen, Mirror reads the owner's Pinterest boards and the pins on them, including their images, titles and board names. Access is read-only: Mirror never creates, edits, saves or deletes boards or pins, and never posts anything to Pinterest.

How the data is used

Pin images are shown on the mirror screen as a slideshow. That is the only use. The data is not analysed, profiled, used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

Where the data is kept

  • Pin images are loaded for display and are not saved, apart from the ordinary short-lived cache of the display software.
  • The Pinterest access token that lets Mirror read the owner's boards is stored only on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner's account.
  • Nothing is sent to any server other than Pinterest's own.

Third parties

Mirror does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or any other third-party services. The only outside service it talks to is Pinterest.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Mirror at any time, either in Mirror's settings or by removing Mirror from the connected apps in Pinterest's settings. When access is removed, the stored token is deleted and Mirror can no longer read any Pinterest data.

Other users

Mirror is not offered to the public, and no one other than its owner signs in to it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/qthinhp/mirror-privacy/issues.

Mirror is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.
